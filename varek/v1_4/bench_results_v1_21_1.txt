  Notes
  - 'added' is the cost of a mediated call: the decision, the notification round trip
    to the Warden, and (for an allowed open or connect) the Warden opening the file or
    dialing the destination for the agent. 'Warden decision' is the Warden's own time
    from its verdict records, without the round trip.
  - Natively the denied and unmatched calls do their full work (the file opens, the
    connection is made); under the Warden they are refused before any of it.
  - An agent slows down by (mediated calls) x (added), so how much depends on how
    often it opens files and connects compared with the work it does in between.
    The request row shows one call next to a small whole request on loopback; a
    real network request takes far longer, so the share there is smaller.
  - These numbers are for this host. Compare hosts by running varek bench on each.

How these compare with figures published earlier
------------------------------------------------
- The v1.14, v1.15 and v1.16 release notes give a P50 of 8 us for "all decisions",
  from bench_target. That is the Warden's own time (latency_us in its records, timed
  from the moment it receives the notification), not what the agent waits, and it is
  a median over bench_target's mix, in which every connect was refused after its
  decision with nothing dialed (v1.9.1's deny-only posture, in force until v1.21.0).
  The "Warden decision" column above is the same measure, split by kind of call:
  13-17 us for a refused open, 56-67 us for an authorized open (v1.15 and
  v1.16 reported 48-51 us for authorized opens alone), and 117-118 us for an allowed
  connect, which since v1.21.0 includes the Warden dialing the destination.
- What the agent waits for a mediated call is the "under the Warden" column: about
  53-80 us for an open and 147-148 us for an allowed connect on this host,
  32-138 us more than the same call natively ("added").
- The per-run p50s under the Warden are in each report ("Warden p50 across runs").
